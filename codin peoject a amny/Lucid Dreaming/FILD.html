FILD

FILD stands for Finger iInduced Lucid dreaming. It is a moderately hard technique.

How to do FILD

  1. First, lie in a comfortable position on our bed (perferably during bedtime or during the night).

  2. while laying down, tap your index and middle finger onto the bed, like playing the piano.

  3. As you continue to tap your fingers onto the bed, decrease the amount you tap until you can barely tell that you are tapping the bed.

  4. continue to tap your fingers as you fll asleep.

  5. enjoy your lucid dream!
